## 封面 v1 初稿

- 用途：模组封面与社区展示。
- 制作日期：2026-10-24。
- 制作方式：内置图像生成工具；未提供游戏截图或第三方图片作为输入。
- 主体：猫咪宇航员、以对话气泡构成的通讯塔、语言字符与轨道。
- 图片文字：`KSA BABEL`、`喵语塔`；语言符号 `A`、`文`、`あ`、`Я`。
- 文件：[ksa-babel-cover-v1.png](ksa-babel-cover-v1.png)。图像文字与主体构图已目视检查。

## 生成提示词

```text
Use case: ads-marketing / stylized-concept.
Asset type: finished square cover artwork for the community game localization mod "KSA Babel", also usable as a mod-manager catalog thumbnail.
Create one original, polished 1:1 square cover, high resolution. This is the actual artwork, not a mockup of packaging or a screen.
Subject: a memorable cream-and-ginger kitten astronaut, head and upper body large enough to read at thumbnail size, triangular cat ears visible inside a clear round space helmet, simple ivory spacesuit, curious confident expression. Behind the kitten, a compact futuristic communications tower made of three stacked speech-bubble-like modules subtly evokes the Tower of Babel. An orbital arc links a few luminous language-symbol panels showing only the individual glyphs "A", "文", "あ", "Я". The panels communicate multilingual translation, with no country flags. Keep the cat, tower and orbital motif cohesive rather than a collage.
Backdrop: deep space, a restrained star field, a softly illuminated planetary horizon. Spacious, uncluttered, strong silhouette. Stylized premium indie space-game key art with crisp graphic shapes, subtly painted surfaces, warm natural light on the cat contrasting with cool space illumination. Friendly and adventurous, not photorealistic, not generic corporate AI imagery.
Composition: strong centered focal silhouette, all important art and type within the central 82 percent of the square so it survives thumbnailing. Cat occupies the upper-middle area; typography has its own clean dark area in the lower quarter. Plenty of clear separation between details.
Text, render exactly: "KSA BABEL" in very large bold clean geometric sans-serif lettering, highly legible. Beneath it, the smaller Chinese title "喵语塔", exactly these three characters. English title should be the dominant type; Chinese should be clean and accurately formed. No other words, no tagline, no version number.
Constraints: no official KSA, KSP or Steam logos, no watermark, no flags, no extra captions, no fake UI, no screenshots, no border or rounded outer corners. The image should feel like one professional mod cover, with the kitten and multilingual orbital communication as the core identity.
```
